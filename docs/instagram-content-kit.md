## 1. Positioning & voice

**One-line positioning**
> India's first app built only for the Banjara community — find your life partner, shop at
> Banjara Bazar, and stay connected with your people.

**What we are**: a trusted, modern home for Gor Banjara people online.
**What we are not**: a dating app, a generic marketplace, a news page.

**Voice**
| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Warm, respectful, family-first | Flirty or "swipe" language |
| Proud of Banjara culture, never touristy about it | Exoticising the dress/jewellery as a costume |
| Plain Hindi + plain English, short sentences | Heavy Sanskritised Hindi or corporate English |
| "our community", "our people", "aapka apna app" | "users", "customers", "leads" |
| Say what the app actually does | Promise matches, guarantees or numbers we can't back |

**Bilingual rule**: English line first, Hindi line second, separated by a line break. Keep the
Hindi line short enough to survive the "... more" truncation on the first 2 lines.

## 2. Visual system

Pull straight from the website so the feed and the site look like one brand.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maroon | `#8B1A2E` | Primary background, buttons, logo lockup |
| Maroon mid | `#B02840` | Gradients, highlights |
| Maroon tint | `#F2D0D5` | Soft backgrounds, quote cards |
| Off-white | `#FFF8F2` | Light posts, text on maroon |
| Near black | `#0A0205` | Dark posts, reel end-cards |

- **Headlines**: Recoleta Bold (the serif on the site). **Body**: Poppins Medium.
- **Grid rhythm**: repeat a 3-post block — `maroon card → photo/reel cover → cream card`.
  Scroll the profile after every post; the grid is the first impression, not the post.
- **Every post ends with**: small logo bottom-right + `banjarahub.com` or `@thebanjarahub`.
- **Reel covers**: one bold Recoleta line, max 5 words, high contrast, safe from the UI
  overlay — keep text inside the middle 60% vertically.
- **Feed posts**: 4:5 (1080×1350). **Reels/Stories**: 9:16 (1080×1920). Never square-crop a
  reel cover — design the cover separately so the grid stays clean.

## 6. Reel scripts

Format: **Hook (0–3s) → Body → CTA**. Keep every reel under 30 seconds unless it's a story.
Put burned-in captions on everything — most of the audience watches on mute.

**R1 — "Three worlds, one app" (15s)**
- 0–3s *(on-screen)*: "One app. Three things your family actually needs."
- 3–7s: Screen-record matrimony profile scroll → text: "Verified Banjara profiles"
- 7–11s: Bazar grid scroll → text: "Buy & sell in your community"
- 11–14s: Community tab → text: "Stay connected"
- 14–15s: Maroon end-card, logo + "banjarahub.com"
- Audio: trending upbeat Indian audio, low volume under a voiceover.

**R2 — "Gor Boli in 15 seconds"**
- Hook: "Do you know this word in our language?"
- Show 3 Gor Boli words, one every 4 seconds, big Recoleta type on maroon.
- CTA: "Comment one word your grandmother used 👇"
- *Highest-saving format we have — run it weekly.*

**R3 — "Getting ready" transition**
- Hook: plain clothes, hand swipe → full traditional phetiya, kanchali and jewellery.
- Text over: "500 years of design. Zero filters."
- CTA: "Shop Lambani work on Banjara Bazar 🔗"

**R4 — "How gotra matching works"**
- Hook: "Why does BanjaraHub ask for your gotra?"
- Body: 3 quick cards — what gotra is · why families ask · how the app filters for it.
- CTA: "Set your preferences in the app."

**R5 — "Profile glow-up" (before/after)**
- Hook: "This profile got no responses. Here's what we changed."
- Split screen: weak profile vs. improved profile, 3 labelled changes.
- CTA: "Go fix yours right now."

**R6 — "2-minute listing"**
- Hook: "You can sell your first product before this reel ends."
- Screen recording, sped up: photo → title → price → post.
- CTA: "Open Banjara Bazar 🔗"

**R7 — Match story (30s)**
- Hook: couple on camera: "We're both Banjara — but we'd never have met."
- Body: how they matched, first message, the families meeting.
- CTA: "Your turn. Link in bio." *(written consent required)*

**R8 — "Things only Banjara families will understand"**
- Listicle of 5, 3 seconds each, warm and funny, nothing mocking.
- CTA: "Which one is your family? 👇" *(built for shares)*

**R9 — Wedding dance**
- Real Banjara dance footage, sound on, no text for the first 2 seconds.
- Text at 3s: "Nobody does a wedding like we do."
- CTA: soft — logo end-card only.

**R10 — Founder piece to camera (45s)**
- Hook: "I built this because I couldn't find one."
- Why the app exists, in plain Hindi. Shot on a phone, no script on screen.
- CTA: "If this is your community too — link in bio."
- *Run this once a quarter. Founder-face content consistently outperforms branded content.*

## 10. Growth plan (first 90 days)

1. **Post the series, not one-offs.** Four fixed weekly slots beat ten random posts.
2. **Reply to every comment within an hour** of posting for the first 3 posts of the week.
3. **Collaborate**, don't just tag: use Instagram's *Collab* feature with Banjara creators,
   artisans and community pages so the post lands on both grids.
4. **Ask for UGC in every story** — wedding photos, Teej photos, Bazar unboxings. Repost
   daily. It's the cheapest content you will ever get and it builds trust fastest.
5. **Run match stories on Fridays without fail.** Nothing converts installs like proof.
6. **Cross-post reels to WhatsApp status and community groups** — that's where the audience
   actually lives.
7. **Drive to the app in the caption, not just the bio**: "Search BanjaraHub on Play Store".

**Track weekly**: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link taps, follower growth,
and installs attributed to Instagram. Saves + shares matter more than likes — they're what
push a post into non-follower feeds.

## 11. Ground rules

- **Consent in writing** before posting any member's photo, name, city or match story.
  Screenshot the permission and keep it. This is a matrimony product — one leaked profile
  costs more trust than a year of good posts buys.
- **Blur other people's faces** in event and wedding photos unless they've agreed.
- **Never publish** a member's phone number, exact address, workplace or full profile card.
- **Don't claim** guaranteed matches, and only use member/match counts the app can back up.
- **Credit the artisan** by name on every Banjara Bazar post, with their permission.
- **Verify festival dates every year** — Teej and Sevalal Jayanti move; don't reuse last
  year's schedule blindly.
- **No political content.** The community spans many states and parties; the account stays
  out of it.
- **Check culture claims with community elders** before posting anything historical. Getting
  our own history wrong in public is the one mistake that's hard to walk back.
